What Rankkiln does

Rankkiln suggests blog topics, writes drafts with an AI model, checks them, and saves them on your platform as unpublished drafts. A draft is a starting point: AI can get facts wrong, so every draft lists claims for you to check. You decide what gets published, unless you switch auto-publish on for a site.
