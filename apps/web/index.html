How it works

From a search query to a draft on your site

  1. 01

    Find topics with demand

    Search Console queries where you sit on page two or three, autocomplete expansions of your keywords, and ideas you add. Topics you've covered are skipped or flagged for a refresh.

  2. 02

    Plan, then write

    A brief first: search intent, outline, questions people ask, links to your own pages. The draft follows your audience, tone and the facts you allow it to state.

  3. 03

    Check everything

    Title and description length, keyword placement, length, links that resolve, banned words. Every draft lists the claims a person should verify.

  4. 04

    Deliver as a draft

    The post lands on your platform unpublished, with an email that links to it. Approve it there or in Rankkiln.

FAQ

Questions

Will Google penalise AI-written posts?

Google ranks helpful content regardless of how it was made, and acts against mass-produced pages written to game rankings. Rankkiln writes to a real search question, to a cadence you set, and a person reviews every post before it goes live.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. Rankkiln is open source under the AGPL-3.0. The command-line version runs with your own API key and a config file.

What does it need from my site?

Your address, so it can propose a profile you then edit, and access to save drafts on your platform. Search Console access is optional and read-only.

Does it write in my voice?

It writes from a profile you control: who you write for, your tone, "I" or "we", words to avoid, and the only facts it may state about your business.
