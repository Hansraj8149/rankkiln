Rankkiln

Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Rankkiln is run by Hansraj Saini. This page says what the hosted service at rankkiln.hansrajsaini.com stores, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. If you self-host Rankkiln, none of this applies: your data stays wherever you run it.

What we store

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send you drafts for review.
  • Your site profiles: the address, audience, tone, keywords and facts you give each site.
  • Platform credentials (for example a WordPress application password or a Ghost Admin key), so Rankkiln can save drafts on your site. They're encrypted before they're stored.
  • Google Search Console access, if you connect it: a refresh token for read-only access, encrypted, and the property you choose.
  • Your own Anthropic API key, if you add one, encrypted. We show only its last four characters.
  • What Rankkiln makes for you: topic ideas, drafts, the checks run on them, and a log of each run with its model, token count and cost.
  • Billing records: your plan and the Lemon Squeezy customer and subscription ids. We never see or store your card.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps you signed in. It lasts 30 days, and signing out removes it. Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage. There's no advertising or tracking cookie and no analytics script.

Google user data

With your permission, Rankkiln reads your Search Console search queries, impressions and positions for the property you choose, and uses them only to suggest topics for that site. It doesn't sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and people don't read it unless you ask for support or the law requires it. Rankkiln's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Search Console for a site at any time, and you can also remove access from your Google account.

Who else processes your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the service and its database.
  • Anthropic writes the briefs and drafts. It receives your site profile, the topic, and text from your site's public pages. With your own key, the request goes to your Anthropic account.
  • Resend sends sign-in links and draft emails.
  • Lemon Squeezy takes payments as the merchant of record.
  • Google provides Search Console data, and autocomplete suggestions for your keywords.
  • Your own platform (WordPress, Ghost, GitHub, Webflow, Shopify or your webhook) receives the drafts.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Everything stays until you delete it. Deleting a site removes its profile, credentials, Search Console access, topics, runs and drafts. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) removes all of it and cancels your subscription. Drafts already saved on your platform stay there. Lemon Squeezy keeps its own payment records, as the law requires.

Your rights

You can see and edit your data in the dashboard, and delete it as described above. To ask for a copy of your data, or for anything else about privacy, email hello@hansrajsaini.com.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll email you before the change takes effect.
